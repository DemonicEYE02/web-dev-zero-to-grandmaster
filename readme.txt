HOW TO START
1. Keep all the files together in one folder.
2. Double-click index.html.
3. The course opens in your browser.

If saving progress does not work when you open index.html directly:
1. Double-click start.bat (Windows), or
2. Run: python serve.py
3. Open http://localhost:8080

HOW IT WORKS
- Your progress saves automatically in your browser.
- Click "Course" to see all 10 worlds.
- Finish missions, pass bosses, then take the final exam.
- Pass the exam to get your certificate.
- Pip, the squishy guide, shows you what to do next.

TIPS
- You do not need the internet.
- Do not delete the assets folder.
- Use Settings to export a backup of your progress.
